Features

Everything you need to master your money

From automatic categorization to AI-powered insights, Spendifi gives you superpowers over your finances.

AI Auto-Categorization

Add an expense and AI instantly categorizes it. Learns your local merchants — from Maxi to Tim Hortons — and gets smarter over time.

Smart Budget Suggestions

AI analyzes 3 months of spending and recommends personalized budget limits. One click to create — no guesswork needed.

Monthly AI Insights Email

Get a beautifully designed monthly report with AI-written spending analysis, trends, and personalized recommendations.

Recurring Expenses

Set up subscriptions, bills, and regular payments once. Spendifi auto-logs them on schedule so you never miss a thing.

Multi-Category Budgets

Group related categories into a single budget. Track "Food" as one limit across Groceries, Restaurants, and Coffee.

Webhook API

Integrate with your bank, Zapier, or custom apps via a simple REST API. Auto-log expenses from any source with your personal API key.
